# Fireline — itch.io page

## Short description (tagline)
Top-down co-op zombie shooter for up to 4 friends: hold the line, build your base, survive the night.

## Description

**Fireline** is a top-down online shooter. Team up with up to three friends against endless zombie
waves, or turn your guns on each other in Deathmatch.

### Game modes
- **Zombie Arena** — fight through city streets, unlock doors, buy perks from the perk machine and
  take down bosses.
- **Base Defense** — raise walls, place gates, traps and mines, train soldiers in barracks and keep
  the headquarters standing through day and night. Blood moons bring the worst of it.
- **Deathmatch** — the player with the most kills wins.

### Features
- Online co-op and PvP for up to 4 players with a simple lobby code
- Three difficulties: Easy, Normal, Nightmare
- Many zombie types: runners, brutes, spitters, exploders, armored zombies and bosses
- Pistol, machine gun, shotgun and sniper rifle, with limited ammo and refills from the shop
- Shop, sell zone and loot: collect, sell, upgrade
- Build turrets, walls (wood, stone, metal), gates, repair stations, barbed wire and mines
- Allied soldiers you can order to follow you or hold position
- Revive downed teammates; see where they fell even off screen
- Levels and perks: more health, faster revives, faster reloads, faster feet
- Turkish and English, switchable in the lobby
- Plays on PC; touch controls on mobile

### Controls (PC)
| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move | WASD / arrow keys |
| Sprint | Left Shift |
| Aim | Mouse |
| Shoot | Left mouse button |
| Reload | R |
| Ping | G or middle mouse button |
| Repair | F |
| Soldiers: follow / hold | H |
| Revive | Press the revive button over a downed teammate |

## Türkçe açıklama

**Fireline**, yukarıdan bakışlı online bir nişancı oyunu. En fazla üç arkadaşınla sonu gelmeyen zombi
dalgalarına karşı birlikte savaş, ya da Deathmatch'te silahları birbirinize çevirin.

### Modlar
- **Zombi Arena** — şehir sokaklarında savaş, kapıları aç, yetenek makinesinden güç al, boss'ları indir.
- **Üs Savunması** — duvar ör, kapı, tuzak ve mayın kur, kışlada asker yetiştir, karargâhı gece
  gündüz ayakta tut. Kanlı ay gecelerinde işler iyice zorlaşır.
- **Deathmatch** — en çok vuran kazanır.

### Özellikler
- Lobi koduyla 4 kişiye kadar online co-op ve PvP
- Üç zorluk: Kolay, Normal, Kâbus
- Koşucu, kaba kuvvet, tükürükçü, patlayan, zırhlı zombiler ve boss'lar
- Tabanca, makineli, pompalı ve keskin nişancı tüfeği; sınırlı mermi, mağazadan ikmal
- Mağaza, satış alanı ve ganimet
- Taret, duvar (tahta, taş, metal), kapı, onarım istasyonu, dikenli tel ve mayın kurma
- Seni takip eden ya da nöbet tutan müttefik askerler
- Düşen arkadaşını dirilt; ekran dışında bile nerede düştüğünü gör
- Seviyeler ve yetenekler
- Türkçe ve İngilizce, lobiden değiştirilebilir
